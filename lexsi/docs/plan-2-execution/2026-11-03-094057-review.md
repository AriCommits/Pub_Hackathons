# Depth-1 transcript routing, chosen-tool arguments, and B2d1 evaluation

Plan 2 adds historical first-call reporting, routes later hops from the rendered transcript, asks the model for arguments after the router chooses a tool, and records first-call metrics in live evaluations. The three sprint commits follow the required D1/C1, A1/M1, and G1 order, with passing local tests and truthful `act` failure records after each sprint. The retained B2d1 output contains all four required metrics and preserves first-tool accuracy at 1.000, but its second turn does not consistently use the inner model as the required finish turn. Watch for: **confirmed** — 42 of the 95 two-call B2d1 episodes route `lookup_record` on the second turn, so the trial does not implement G1's one-routed-tool-then-finish protocol.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

D1 reconstructs the saved B0/B1/B2 first-call rates without a GPU run, including B2 at 1.000 first-tool accuracy and 0.894 first-argument accuracy on 104 cases. M1 adds strict first-call name and exact-argument metrics to episode scoring, live aggregation, and comparison output.

C1 changes the router input after the first hop from the original task to the rendered transcript. A1 reuses the live model generator to fill arguments for the selected tool, folds the one-tool instruction into the final user turn to satisfy Tiny Aya's alternating-role template, and retains heuristic fallback for invalid or cross-tool generations.

G1 applies run-specific step and replica limits and executes B2d1 on one physical CUDA device 1 replica. **Confirmed:** the two-step cap does not force the second step through the inner finish policy; the same routed policy runs again and 42 retained episodes select `lookup_record` on that turn.

The branch has one commit per sprint in the required order. Every recorded local suite passes, every `act` attempt is reported as failed during setup because Docker was unavailable, all 15 current protected-artifact hashes match the recorded values, and the experiment-status change is append-only.

## B2d1 reroutes the second turn instead of forcing the inner finish policy

**Confirmed.** `build_live_project` applies `max_agent_steps: 2`, but it builds the same routed policy used by ordinary B2. `ReActStrategy` invokes that policy for both allowed steps. On step 1, `routed_policy` routes another top-1 tool and calls the inner model only after abstention or an identical successful repeat.

G1 requires one router-selected first call followed by one inner-model finish turn. The retained `results/b0_b2/B2d1/eval.json` has 9 one-call rows and 95 two-call rows; the second calls contain 53 `finish` calls and 42 `lookup_record` calls. Add a B2d1-specific first-hop routing mode, or an equivalent wrapper, that delegates every step after step 0 to the inner policy. Then add a CPU test for that boundary, rerun B2d1, and update the generated metrics and status pointer.
